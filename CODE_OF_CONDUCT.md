# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Technical disagreement should focus on code,
evidence and tradeoffs, not personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse,
unwanted sexual attention and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Do not pressure contributors to run unreviewed automation against personal accounts,
share credentials, or claim verification they did not perform. Reproducible bug reports
and honest negative findings are welcome. Credit prior work and preserve licenses.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation to protect the community.
No permanent maintainer roster or moderation contact is established by this archive. The
publisher must identify responsible people and an actual private reporting route before
opening a public community. The same conduct applies to project-controlled issue trackers,
review discussions and other official spaces once they exist.
