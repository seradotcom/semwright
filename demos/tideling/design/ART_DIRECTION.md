# TIDELING — a small life in a vast reef

One intimate 2.5D reef, a 180-second ascent through three growth stages.
The fish is the emotional anchor: apricot body, butter belly, ink-blue saddle,
large lucid eyes and split, translucent teal fins. A leaf-shaped dorsal crest
and crescent tail carry the silhouette across juvenile, adolescent and mature forms.
No realistic teeth, no borrowed character design, no clinical or educational claims.

Palette: deep water #062B40, open water #167E90, sea glass #77C9BE,
apricot #FFAE70, warm sand #EAD5AD, plum coral #845772.
Warm fish against cool water. Threats are elongated, angular and desaturated;
edible fish are rounded and warm. Size, behavior and a restrained target mark
communicate the food chain together; color alone never carries eligibility.

Camera looks along +Y in Blender / -Z in Godot; gameplay is X/Y in Godot.
Hero faces +X. Blender Z-up becomes Godot Y-up via glTF.
Layered reef silhouettes frame an open swimming corridor. Foreground fronds
remain near the edges. Sparse suspended particles, moving light ribbons,
sand ripples, coral fans and distant schooling provide depth without an ocean simulation.

Typography: quiet sans-serif, generous spacing. Title and ending can be editorial;
play HUD only needs growth, combo, time and dash recharge. Growth is a camera breath,
light pulse and a new silhouette, never a LEVEL UP modal.

Wave order: 0 direction → 1 hero → 2 reef → 3 movement → 4 core loop →
5 ecosystem → 6 polish → 7 golden game → 8 semantic surfaces → 9 cross-app →
10 clean merged-SHA public proof. Each phase needs its own observed evidence.
A prototype render is not an approved golden game or a driver proof.
