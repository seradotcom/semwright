# TIDELING
Original desktop 2.5D reef survival game, WASD/arrows or left stick, Space/controller A burst.
A curious small fish eats smaller fish, grows through three stages and survives a 180-second reef.
Audience: anyone who can understand swimming and the food chain in five seconds; also viewers
of a verifiable Semwright Blender → Godot creative pipeline. One reef, seven baseline species,
combo, dash, three growth stages. An eighth BlueGoldFish is reserved for the cross-app demonstration.
The game itself must stand alone. Authoring and proof controls never appear in the shipping UI.
No world map, quests, inventory, crafting, multiplayer or backend. Preserve all adjacent projects.
